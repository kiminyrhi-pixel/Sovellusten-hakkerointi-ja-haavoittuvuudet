# Freedom of Action, Control, and Risk Mitigation

## Define the ISMS scope for your home network and study lab

### a1) What is included in the scope

- My home network infrastructure, including the router, Wi-Fi, printer, NAS, and relevant IoT devices.
- The devices I use for the course exercises, including my laptop or workstation and virtual machines.
- My phone when it is used for MFA.
- Course materials, personal notes, repositories, lab materials, credentials, and cryptographic keys.
